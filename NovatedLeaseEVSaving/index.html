Assumptions & method

What the result means. Today's value of buying outright and running the car, less today's value of 60 lease payments, running costs, fees and the final cash buyout. Both options end with the same car; its resale value cancels.

Monthly cost. After tax, including running costs and lease fees. Total lease cash cost spread over 60 months. This average is undiscounted and excludes the final buyout and transfer fees.

Tax. 32% = 30% income tax + 2% Medicare; 39% = 37% + 2%. These flat rates apply to all salary sacrifice unless income is entered. Income mode calculates the before/after resident tax difference, including bracket changes and the low income tax offset (LITO). It uses five full annual tax periods and constant income; part-year starts are not modelled. Taxable income after sacrifice must remain at least $45,000. Full Medicare levy assumed; no surcharge, HELP, other benefit tests or super changes.

GST & finance. Automatic purchase GST saving is one-eleventh of the price excluding non-GST charges, capped at $6,353 (2026–27). Finance covers the remaining price plus establishment fees. Rental and admin GST credits are fully passed through. Insurance/CTP use only the GST entered from invoices; stamp duty earns no GST credit. Public charging and maintenance assume full GST credits. Home charging uses substantiated actual cost with no assumed GST credit. Quote overrides take priority.

Running costs. Both options use the same competitively sourced prices. Self-arranged insurance is reimbursed pre-tax where the provider allows it; untick reimbursement if unavailable. First-year registration/CTP is already in the drive-away price; only four renewals are added. Costs rise by the selected annual percentage and are spread monthly. Reimbursement delays and annual-payment timing differences are not modelled.

Buyout. Default residual: 28.13% of the vehicle cost after purchase GST savings, excluding establishment fees. Add 10% GST and entered buyout/transfer charges; pay after tax at month 60. Enter the quote's GST-inclusive residual when available.

Discounting. Discount rate = lease rate − 2 percentage points. Converted to effective monthly discounting. This still applies when quoted repayments override the finance calculation. Rates below 2% produce a negative discount rate. No extra investment return is added.

Scope. Full FBT exemption for all five years, continuous salary packaging and private use. Treasury has proposed concession changes from 2027; confirm the contract retains full exemption. Shared home-charger costs cancel. No early termination, future tax-law changes or unentered fees are assumed. Default costs are illustrations.

Sources: ATO EV exemption · GST · Residuals · Tax brackets · LITO · 2026–27 car limit · Insurance reimbursement · Proposed FBT changes
